# smart-home-devices
For my UnEssay, I chose to inform consumers about the security of smart home devices they may purchase for their homes. The format I chose was to develop an informative website where consumers could learn more about the security vulnerabilities in 10 different devices. The devices are categorized into three sections: Assistant, Security, and Service. Each device has data and information on its CVE program-reported vulnerabilities. The website is made to keep consumers informed and engaged. 


“A Review of Cyber Security Challenges, Attacks and Solutions for Internet of Things Based Smart Home.” IJCSNS International Journal of Computer Science and Network Security, vol. 19, no. 9, Sept. 2019. ResearchGate, PDF Link

This journal article is focused on detailing the different types of cyber attacks that smart home devices are vulnerable to because they’re connecting to the internet. It details the types of vulnerabilities, what they do, how they are used, and what solutions are available for consumers to secure their devices from these vulnerabilities. It categorizes the attackers into 8 different categories of cybercriminals. The malware discussed and the methodologies that the malware is used to attack smart home systems.

	I will use the categories of cybercriminals to place labels on who might be trying to attack the different smart home devices, and in what way. The methodologies will be useful, so I can inform the consumer of what’s happening, so they can better understand. I won’t be going as in-depth with the technologies, but simplifying them, so that the consumer knows what they’re up against. The solutions against these attacks will be useful as recommendations for the consumer to use to defend their homes against cybersecurity threats.

	I believe this journal article to be credible because it uses over 40 references from sources such as Cisco, IEEE, and IBM.
